Custom order process

1. Share your idea

Send us a description, preferred materials and colours, dimensions where relevant, quantity, and any reference images.

2. Design review and estimate

Our team reviews the request and may ask follow-up questions. Any initial estimate is indicative and depends on the confirmed design and materials.

3. Final quotation and approval

We’ll issue a final quotation for your approval before production. The approved design and agreed price form the basis of the custom order.

4. Deposit and production

Production begins after the required deposit and design approval are confirmed. The deposit amount, cancellation terms and production timeline are agreed with you before work starts.

5. Updates and delivery

We’ll share progress updates and arrange delivery or collection when your piece is ready.

Deposit percentage, cancellation and refund rules, and production timelines remain to be set by the business.
