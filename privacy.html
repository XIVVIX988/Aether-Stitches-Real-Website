Privacy policy

We respect the trust you place in Aether Stitches when you shop with us or share an idea. This policy explains the information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, delivery address, order information, payment status and messages you send us. For custom requests, this can include design notes and reference images you choose to share.

2. How we use your information

  • To process orders and arrange delivery.
  • To respond to questions and custom requests.
  • To keep records of payments and customer service.
  • To improve our products and website experience.

3. Sharing and security

We share relevant details only with service providers involved in completing your order, such as delivery and payment providers. We use reasonable safeguards to protect customer information and limit access to authorized staff.

4. Your choices

You can contact us to ask about the personal information we hold or to correct your details. Contact aetherstitches@gmail.com .

This starter policy is a draft for the website. Final retention, privacy and customer data practices should be confirmed by the business.
